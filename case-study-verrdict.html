DECISIONS

Five calls I can defend

DECISION 01 / 05

Tell the reviewer what to open first

A short ranked list of four, ordered by business impact and deadline.

Instead of

Better filters, or sorting the table by severity.

Why

452 open alerts. Filters only help someone who already knows what to look for.

The cost

They trust a ranking they did not set, so the rule is printed under the list.

DECISION 02 / 05

Show the working, not just the verdict

Every verdict opens into the checks, controls and records behind it.

Instead of

A methodology document, or a tooltip on the badge.

Why

Nobody signs off on a status they cannot explain to someone else.

The cost

Denser rows, and every status needs its evidence stored.

DECISION 03 / 05

Say where every number came from

Each feed states external or internal, live or snapshot, and when it last ran.

Instead of

One "updated 2 minutes ago" stamp in the header.

Why

A vendor advisory and our own inventory are not equally authoritative.

The cost

More small labels, which is why colour stays reserved for risk.

DECISION 04 / 05

Warn eighteen months before the deadline

Alerts start 18 months out and step up in urgency as the date closes in.

Instead of

Alerting on the EOL date, or just showing the date.

Why

Replacing a device takes approvals, build, testing and a release window.

The cost

452 open alerts instead of a handful. Only workable because the queue is ranked.

DECISION 05 / 05

Let the reviewer act without leaving the record

Raise the Jira ticket from the verdict, pre-filled with the evidence — and track it back to closed.

Instead of

Exporting a list and raising the ticket by hand.

Why

The decision and the fix lived in different tools, so evidence got retyped and the reasoning stayed behind.

The cost

Ticket state can disagree with ours, so every record names the system it last heard from.

THE LOOP

From verdict to closed, without leaving the record

01

Verdict reached

The check runs and the reason is attached to the record.

02

Ticket raised

Sent to Jira pre-filled with records, controls and a link back.

03

Tracked in place

Assignee, status and age read back from Jira, shown on the record.

04

Fix confirmed

Status returns and the record closes against its own evidence.

05

Trail kept

Reason, action and outcome sit on one object for the audit.

Before this, steps two to five happened in other people's tools, and nobody could reconstruct them afterwards.

SEVERITY FRAMEWORK

Time left becomes a severity. Severity names the next action.

Low 12–18 mo Review and roadmap it
Medium 6–12 mo Plan: options, owner, effort
High 3–6 mo Approve and raise the work
Critical 0–3 mo Escalate and confirm the plan
Overdue passed Remediate or except it

EOS end of support · EOL end of life · EOVS end of vendor support. A way of ranking work that I proposed, not a published policy.

INTERACTION & STATES

Designed for the states nobody demos

Empty

Zero is an achievement. "0 fully compliant · no change vs last 7 days" still reports the comparison.

Loading

Refresh is per source. One feed can scan while the rest stay readable. The screen never blanks.

Stale

Age is always on screen. Last run per feed, last sync per integration. Stale is labelled, not hidden.

Critical

One banner, never two. Only the single most urgent breach gets the top slot.
