AK — constructed marks.

Drawn, not set. A shared-stem ligature, letters cut out of a solid, a calligraphic swash, folded facets, a seal and an offset-print build. Each is shown at 96, 40 and 28px on both grounds — the 28px column is the one that decides it.

The ligature is the idea worth noticing. In options 1, 5 and 6 the A's right leg and the K's stem are the same stroke — the two letters are genuinely fused rather than placed side by side. That is what makes a monogram a monogram.
