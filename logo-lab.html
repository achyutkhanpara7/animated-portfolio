AK monogram — six directions.

Every mark is live CSS or SVG on your existing tokens, so it scales to any size, inverts for dark, and animates without an image file. Each is shown at 96px (hero), 40px (nav) and 28px (favicon) on both grounds — small sizes are where monograms usually fail.

Reading the small sizes. A mark that only works at 96px is not a logo. Check the 28px column first — that is your favicon and your avatar. All six respect prefers-reduced-motion.
